Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to give up eating Indian food?

Absolutely not. Our nutritional frameworks are built around eating home-cooked meals including roti, rice, and dal. We focus on macros and balance, not restriction.

I travel often for work. Will this still work for me?

Yes. We provide specific 'dining-out' and 'travel' frameworks so you know exactly how to order at restaurants and hotels without breaking your progress.

Do I need a gym membership?

It is optional. We customize the workout plans based on what you have available, whether that is a full gym, dumbbells at home, or purely bodyweight.
